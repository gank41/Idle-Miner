# Idle Miner 1.9.2 (build 14)

## A simpler update window

When you are up to date, the window shows installed/latest versions, a short status and **Close**. Optional **Release Notes** start collapsed; click to expand or hide them. Available updates show their changes before **Download and Install Now**. The Mac and Linux windows reset their details between checks.

Signed installation, wallet settings, currency restoration and login preferences keep their existing behavior. The older 1.9 updater may still reopen stopped for its first update; automatic restoration is supported for updates initiated by 1.9.1 and later.
